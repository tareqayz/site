I'm Tareq. This is my training log. Every day I write down what I trained, why I did it, and where I learned it.

The goal is simple to say and hard to do: become a superhuman athlete, **the unorthodox way**. Not one sport, not one muscle, but all eight things a body can do: strength, power, endurance, cardio, flexibility, mobility, balance and agility.

Every bead in the mala above is a day. The filled ones are days I showed up. Some will stay hollow. That's part of it.
